AIM720 vs Allen for NEET Droppers: Honest Comparison 2027

Which coaching is actually better for your drop year? Real analysis based on faculty, fees, doubt resolution & success metrics.

Published: August 02, 2026 | Updated: 2027 Edition

If you've taken a drop year for NEET 2027, choosing the right coaching institute can make the difference between a 550 and a 650+ score. Two names that consistently appear in dropper conversations are Padhle AIM720 and Allen. Both have strong market presence, but they serve droppers very differently. This comparison cuts through the marketing noise and gives you the actual facts you need to decide.

1. Curriculum Structure & Chapter Prioritization for Droppers

Droppers aren't beginners—they've already covered Biology twice and Chemistry formulas multiple times. The coaching you choose must acknowledge this and focus on high-yield topics and conceptual depth rather than basic repetition.

Padhle AIM720 Approach

AIM720 is specifically designed for dropper batches. Their curriculum explicitly prioritizes chapters that carry maximum weightage in recent NEET papers: Genetics & Evolution (Biology), Thermodynamics & Electrochemistry (Chemistry), and Modern Physics (Physics). The teaching methodology assumes you've already seen these topics and focuses on question patterns, MCQ traps, and numerical problem-solving strategies. Modules are organized around NEET syllabus sections, not textbook chapters, which saves droppers the time of cherry-picking important topics themselves.

Allen's Approach

Allen runs the same curriculum for both first-time takers and droppers, though they do have separate batches. This means you'll cover foundational concepts even if you've mastered them. While thorough, this approach wastes precious time a dropper doesn't have. Allen covers 100% of NCERT thoroughly, which is important, but doesn't explicitly shortcut to dropper-specific problem solving.

💡 Critical Insight for Droppers

Your second attempt is about accuracy and speed, not learning basics. You need a curriculum that respects your previous preparation and jumps directly to advanced MCQ patterns, not one that assumes you're starting fresh. AIM720 is built for exactly this timeline.

2. Faculty Quality & Doubt Resolution (Real Criteria)

Coaching fame often rests on 1-2 star teachers, but you won't always get them. What matters for droppers is consistent, accessible faculty who understand why you're struggling on specific questions.

Padhle AIM720

AIM720 focuses on active doubt resolution through live sessions. Their faculty conducts doubt-clearing classes 4-5 times per week where you can ask questions in real-time. Since it's a smaller, dropper-focused program, faculty members actually know their students' weak areas. Response time to doubts posted on their platform is typically 4-8 hours. Faculty includes NEET-scoring doctors and experienced educators who specifically teach droppers—they understand the psychology of a second attempt.

Allen

Allen has excellent faculty (names like Ajay Mohan for Chemistry are genuine), but with their massive student base (100,000+), individual attention becomes scarce. Doubt resolution happens through offline centers and online portals, but with class sizes of 100-200 students per batch, personalized guidance is limited. A dropper often doesn't get direct access to the star faculty you see in ads.

The Dropout Reality Check

Droppers often struggle with motivation and need faculty who can provide that emotional support alongside academics. AIM720's smaller batch model naturally enables this; Allen's scale works against it. If you're the type who needs to call and talk through a problem, AIM720's accessibility wins decisively.
